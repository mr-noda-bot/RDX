3. Axioms:
Primitive 1 — Events
Discrete occurrences. They have no size, no shape, no coordinates, and no intrinsic properties except the ability to relate to other events.
Primitive 2 — Precedence Relation
A binary relation that says one event comes before another. It does not specify a duration, a distance, or a coordinate—only the ordering.
Primitive 3 — Local Finiteness
Between any two events that can be ordered, there are only finitely many intermediate events. This prevents infinite density and enforces discrete structure.
Primitive 4 — Operational Identifiability
Every event and every ordering judgment is something that can be established by a concrete observational or experimental procedure.
Primitive 5 — No Background Structure
Nothing like space, time, coordinates, metric, or manifold is assumed. Only events and their ordering exist at the foundational level.
Primitive 6 — Statistical Uniformity for Continuum Emergence
On large enough scales, the distribution of events is statistically consistent so that continuum-like behavior can emerge when coarse-grained.
