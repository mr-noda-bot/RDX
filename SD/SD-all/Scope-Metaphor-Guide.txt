---

2. Interpretation

Brain → Local/Region/Global/Universal:
Governs adaptive decisions and emergent computation at multiple scales. Local neurons fire, regional subnetworks propagate, global layers enforce system patterns, and universal intelligence handles cross-system feedback.

Differential → Local/Region/Global/Universal:
Governs mechanical balancing. Local torque adjustments affect a single node, regional gear sets distribute torque among pools, global gear assemblies balance overall lattice, universal rotation ensures consistent system-wide spin.

Government → Local/Region/Global/Universal:
Enforces constraints, policy, and law. Local councils handle small pools, districts coordinate regions, federal agencies manage the global state, and universal principles enforce invariants across multiple systems.

Loom → Local/Region/Global/Universal:
Handles pattern propagation. Single warp/weft for local node interactions, pooled batches for regional patterns, full panel weaving for global structure, and multi-looms for universal context alignment.

4. Operational Insight

1. Tasks & Flows:

Execution lives mostly in Loom + Differential, moving from local → universal.



2. Directing & Governance:

Decision-making lives mostly in Brain + Government, also scaled across scopes.



3. Duality / Multi-Scale:

Local/Global dual-scope nodes operate at intersections, e.g., a local loom weave may influence a regional differential gear alignment, guided by a brain policy decision.
