RULE-01  /  DUAL CHANNEL PRESERVATION
Dual-mapped positions are never reduced to one channel by the operator. Reduction, if it occurs, is a system output — logged as such, not assumed. A native derivative, when and where it arises, is dual-tracked. Reduction in a derivative is visible in the ledger.
RULE-02  /  SYSTEM IDENTITY
System identity is self-native. It is not conferred by operation, assignment, or observation. It precedes mapping and persists through reduction. Any operation on the system operates within the identity, not on it from outside.
RULE-03  /  CAUSAL DIRECTIONALITY
Bidirectionality is structural; causal direction is a boundary-condition constraint. Reverse channel may influence forward. Forward does not write back to reverse. This is a constraint on coupling, not on the algebra. Logged, not assumed.

RULE-04  /  GENERATION EVENT
At the threshold of each cycle, the system produces 1n+11 from Pnmax. Position resets. Dimension increments. Origin holds. The semantic field re-anchors at 1 at the new dimensional level.
RULE-05  /  ROLLOVER vs GENERATION
When IntMap(n) = 1, the event is a rollover producing a fold-remainder of 1. This is not closure. The digit-sum process does not divide — it accumulates and folds. The 1 that emerges is the residue of recursive accumulation crossing a base boundary. The recursion re-encounters the generator by arriving from the other side of the boundary, not by returning from closure. Direction of propagation is carried by the dimensional channel (d), not by reclassifying the event.
RULE-06  /  RECURSIVE ALIGNMENT
A rollover is an instantiated instance of recursion tracking alignment to itself under closure. It is not classified as closure OR generation exclusively — both channels are present simultaneously. The system does not decide which it is at the annotation level. The ledger tracks the alignment.
